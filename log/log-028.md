# Log 028 — Pre-move: Enter in terminal A moves the arm 10 cm forward for wrist tests (2026-11-01)

## Why (Suraj: "can you put it in that position, I will only test roll and pitch")
log-027: at the start pose the left wrist has only 14° for pitch up and 30° for roll right; 10 cm forward
every direction has ≥ 27°. Moving there by hand was hard to judge.

## Choice
- Triggered by a person (Enter in terminal A, after the spotter says "arm may move"), never automatic:
  motion without a squeeze must be announced (spotter card).
- Joint-space move of the HOLD target (same mechanism as the record 'a' key, `move_toward`), **10°/s**
  (teleop is 45°/s). Only in HOLD with the follower linked; a squeeze cancels it. Max 15 cm.
- Target = IK of start gripper + 10 cm forward with the **same gripper rotation**; validated like every packet.

## Code
- `pico_teleop/sender.py` `ArmChannel.premove_forward(fwd_m, now, dps=10)`: checks HOLD / link / reachability /
  validator, sets `self.move` and `self.move_dps`; `tick()` moves at `move_dps`, prints `pre-move: arrived`.
- `--premove-forward-cm` (0..15) + a stdin thread in `main()`; `scripts/arm_session.sh` passes `PREMOVE_CM`.
- Tests (`tests/test_follower_feedback.py`): both arms arrive 10 cm forward ±2 mm, rotation < 1°, never
  faster than 10°/s; refused while engaged. Suite **104 passed**.
