  the right controller sits at XR **+x** of the left one in 100 % of samples (+16 cm) → +x = right.
- Root cause: the operator moved to the RIGHT in those trials and in the head turn (said so at the
  time); the frames and the recenter were fine. The matrix is `frames.py` `R_ROBOT_FROM_XR`

  a commit. The left controller's "left" row (hand y +33) was moved back while engaged; redo it
  cleanly before the first arm session.
- Lesson: never decide a frame from a human "left/right" alone; use a geometric fact (the right
  controller is right of the left one) or a marked target in the room (README axis-check section).
