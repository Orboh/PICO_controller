## Finding: the head-angle (facing) guard blocks normal operation
- In every run the operator **turned the head ~45-58° to the left when squeezing**: looking at the real
  arm beside him. The facing guard (headset yaw, limit 30°, checked continuously) refused to engage.
- The guard measures the HEAD; the arm's motion is computed from the controller's position in the
  fixed XR frame, which does not change when the head turns. Only a BODY turned away (the −100° case,
  log-016) makes the directions wrong. So the head is a poor proxy and the guard is too strict.
- Interim: `MAX_FACING_DEG=75` for `gate4_sender.sh` (CLI `--max-facing-deg`).
- **Proposed change (to decide, Suraj):** check facing only at the moment of engaging, limit ~60-75°,
  and while engaged only warn (no FAULT). Still catches a body turned ~100° away.
