## Gate 3 evidence (`bash scripts/test.sh -q` → 56 passed, gate 3 tests ~28 s)
- Receiver `connect()` accepted the first packet: all 8 keys, equal to the pose-set start pose exactly.
- Scenario (idle → engage + circle → release while the hand moves → re-engage elsewhere → all-zero
  pose → release → idle): sender events ENGAGED, released, FAULT (pose zero), fault cleared; seq
  strictly increasing; receiver outputs finite, **max step 0.9° per 5 ms sample** (limit 1.8°).
- Sender stops → receiver goes stale and **holds exactly the last sent action** (no limp, no jump).
- **NaN check:** after a valid first packet, the real receiver accepted `{"joint_3.pos": NaN}` and
  output NaN (garbage strings were dropped). Confirms log-002 finding 1: validation must be sender-side.
- Partial first packet (missing keys) → receiver `connect()` refuses: good receiver behaviour.
- Record 'a' key: receiver `soft_move_to(start + 8° on joint_1)` → sender moved its HOLD target at
  45°/s, "arrived" event, receiver returned True.
- **Receiver finding:** `soft_move_to` returns once the stream is within 5° and then hands out the
  latest target with no ramp (`openarm_leader_remote.py` soft_move_to: `_returned = _target`): a step
  of up to about 4° in one cycle. Same in leader mode; reported, not changed.

## The anchor gap (blocks hardware)
Goal: anchor on the actual or independently verified follower pose. Today:
- The sender's first packet and HOLD target come from the pose-set YAML; the follower aligns to the
  same YAML in `connect()` (sags 5-6° under gravity per the lerobot config comment).
- `lerobot-teleoperate` connects the **receiver first** (waits for our first packet), then the
  follower aligns for about 2.2 s without reading the stream. If the operator engages during those 2.2 s, the
  target moves and the follower **jumps** to it after alignment. `lerobot-record` may connect both
  in parallel.
- Options: (A) operating rule: start the follower, wait for "ready", only then squeeze (no code);
  (B) **receiver sends the follower's measured joints on the echo** (`proposals/lerobot-follower-
  feedback.patch`, about 10 lines, backward compatible) and the sender refuses to engage unless every
  joint is within 8° of the held target, and FAULTs on a tracking error > 8° (safety protocol §1 L0).
  Sender side of (B) is implemented and tested; it is inactive until the receiver sends `follower`.
- **Recommendation: (B)**, owner Kento (lerobot fork). Until then, hardware only with (A), the
  explicit `--confirm-follower-at-start-pose` flag, and a spotter.
