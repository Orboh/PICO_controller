# Log 030 — WALL instead of FAULT at the workspace edge (no lock from one edge contact) (2026-11-01)

## Why (Suraj: "if these things happen while collecting data it will be very painful; find a way to fix it")
Four LOCKs today with one pattern (log-025 stretched arm, log-027 joint_6, log-029 joint_2): edge → FAULT →
re-squeeze in the same pose → the first move toward the edge FAULTs again → LOCKED.

## Design, reviewed first (Elephant + Goldfish on the design, scratchpad wall-design.md)
- Edge checks become a WALL (`controller.py` `_edge`): joint within 3° of its limit and moving toward it; arm
  stretched (sigma < 0.03 and getting smaller); joint past its drift limit (120° j1 / 90°) and moving further;
  joint > 60° from where you squeezed and moving further; target out of reach (Elephant #3: it fires first at the edge).
- At a wall (`_hit_wall`): the new IK answer is not used (target holds, state ENGAGED, joints keep easing to the
  last accepted answer: up to 10°), the anchors slip so the hand maps onto the held target (position
  `anchor_hand = hand − (p_target − anchor_tcp)/scale`; rotation `anchor_tcp_rot · R_targetᵀ · hand_rot`, Elephant
  checked the math). One message per contact ("WALL: …"), contact ends after 0.5 s free (no chatter).
- Each cycle is judged on its own, so sideways / outward moves work at a wall (Goldfish #1).
- Kept as FAULTs (at first): input, hand/rotation jumps, lags, IK jump, rotation gap, link. **Later changed: IK jump → wall (030b), rotation lag and gap → wall (log-033).** **Hard floor: sigma < 0.02 FAULTs**
  (Goldfish #3). No rotation slip while the position-only fallback runs, so the "wrist cannot reach" timer is kept
  (Elephant #1). Lockout unchanged.
- Evidence (Goldfish #4/#5): `wall` per cycle in the sender JSONL; wall time and hand slip printed at exit.
- Deferred: limit avoidance in wrist mode (Elephant #5: 1 redundant DOF, little help on joint_6/7, new FAULT risk).
  Not possible: wall signal in the headset (no vibration in the SDK binding).
