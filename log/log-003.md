## Options and choices
| Question | Chosen | Why |
|---|---|---|
| How to detect a lost controller | all-zero / origin pose **and** a frozen pose while the timestamp moves | the SDK keeps old values; a side missing from the packet just freezes (log-002 §5) |
| Move check | p95 − p5 per axis | one reacquisition jump cannot fake a move (Elephant 7) |
| Sides swapped? | one-side-only phases; the other side must stay < 3 cm | a swap would send to the wrong arm port |
| Menu buttons | reported, not required | the PICO system may reserve them; unverified |
| Stream thresholds | ≥ 30 Hz, no gap > 0.25 s in motion phases | 0.25 s is the receiver's hold threshold |
| Tracking loss | required, deliberately caused (hide each controller, headset off) | gate 1 asks to document it |
