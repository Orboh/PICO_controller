# Log 012 — Gripper "gentle sync" replaces the soft takeover (2026-10-31)

## Why
Arm session 1 (log-010): the gripper only followed the trigger after a near-full pull (soft takeover
needed the mapped trigger within 3° of the gripper; the gripper starts closed at 0°, the trigger is
normally released = −60°). In the online test (log-009) the trigger peaked at 0.64 → nothing happened.

## Options considered
| Option | Result |
|---|---|
| keep the soft takeover | works, but the operator must remember "pull fully once" |
| relative takeover | **rejected**: closed gripper + released trigger → can never open (dead spot) |
| follow immediately at 120°/s | the closed gripper snaps open in 0.5 s right after engaging |
| **gentle sync** (chosen, Suraj) | move toward the trigger's angle at **30°/s** until within 3°, then follow at 120°/s |

## What changed, where
- `pico_teleop/controller.py` `TeleopConfig.gripper_sync_dps = 30.0`; `ArmTeleop.step()` end: speed is
  `gripper_sync_dps` until `grip_synced`, then `max_gripper_speed_dps`. Re-armed at every engage.
  Gripper still moves only while ENGAGED (HOLD/FAULT keep the angle).
- Tests: `test_gripper_gentle_sync` (closed → open at exactly 30°/s, monotonic, then 120°/s after the
  match), `test_gripper_resync_after_each_engage`. Full suite **72 passed**.
- README Controls row "Trigger" rewritten.
